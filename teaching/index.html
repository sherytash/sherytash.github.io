Teaching

I teach what I research: how managers and researchers can work with AI, and where its answers can be trusted. At WU Vienna I teach Agentic AI in Strategy & Innovation, Strategy and Innovation, the Vienna Innovation Program and the Business Project with Vienna Airport; at LMU Munich I taught the Marketing Management exercises, the Seminar on AI in Marketing and the project course with BavariaDirekt.

I have supervised more than fifteen theses at LMU Munich and WU Vienna and run the thesis track Applied AI in Strategy & Innovation.
